ALGORITHM: POCKET-MONEY ANALYZER

1. Start the program.

2. Enter the total pocket money.

3. Create two empty lists:
   - expenses
   - categories

4. Repeat 5 times:
   a. Enter the expense category.
   b. Enter the expense amount.
   c. Store the category in the categories list.
   d. Store the amount in the expenses list.

5. Display all expense categories and amounts.

6. Find the highest expense.

7. Find the lowest expense.

8. Find the category with the highest expense.

9. Count the number of expenses that are ₹200 or more.

10. Calculate the total expense.

11. Calculate the remaining pocket money.

12. Calculate the average expense.

13. Sort the expenses from lowest to highest.

14. Compare the remaining money with the budget and display
    an appropriate spending status.

15. Display the final money report.

16. Stop the program.
